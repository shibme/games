How to play

  • Reveal cells and use the numbers to find every mine. A number says how many mines touch that cell.
  • The first cell you reveal, and every cell touching it, is always safe.
  • Long-press, right-click, or press F to flag a mine, or switch the Reveal / Flag control under the board. Arrow keys move between cells, and Enter acts on the selected cell.
  • Reveal a completed number to open the remaining cells around it.
  • The timer starts on the first reveal, pauses while this tab is hidden, and stops when the board ends.
  • A copied link rebuilds the same minefield. It does not include your flags, time, or progress.
