Tango

Level 1 · easy
Fill the board: 3 suns and 3 moons in every row and column.
Rules
  • Each row and column has 3 suns and 3 moons.
  • No three identical symbols may sit in a line.
  • Cells with = between them must match.
  • Cells with × between them must differ.
  • Shaded starting cells stay locked. Tap an empty cell to cycle sun → moon → empty.
